---
title: "The semantic map"
---

The map turns a vector into a zone id and is identical on every node.

- **Structure**: `coarse` (256) centroids, each with `fine` (256) centroids: 65 536 zones.
  `zone_id = coarse_index * fine + fine_index`.
- **Assignment**: nearest coarse centroid by cosine, then nearest fine centroid inside it.
  A vector also belongs to the second-best zone when `sim2 >= sim1 - 0.02`.
- **Search probe**: the `nprobe` (4) nearest zones among the two nearest coarse groups, plus 2 decoy zones.
- **File** `map-v<N>.bin`: `"ALPMAP01" | version u32 LE | dim u16 | coarse u16 | fine u16 | coarse f16[] | fine f16[]`,
  all centroids L2-normalised. The blake3 of the file is pinned in the software.
- **Construction**: hierarchical spherical k-means (k-means++ seeding, Lloyd iterations) on a large multilingual
  corpus of profile-like sentences, fixed seed, with `grid-mapbuild`. Rebuildable by anyone.
- **Model**: `alp-embed-v1`, 384 dimensions, L2-normalised, cosine similarity. Reference text prefix: none
  (guardians and searchers embed identical text identically).

Dual-version windows are described in [Versions](/governance/versions).
