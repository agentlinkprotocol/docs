---
title: "The transparency ledger"
---

Public facts of the network go to an append-only log inspired by Certificate Transparency.

- Entries chain by `prev_hash`; the hash of an entry is `blake3("alp-ledger-v1" || cbor(entry without witnesses))`.
- Witnesses sign that hash; at least one valid witness is required to import an entry.
- The **root** is the RFC 6962 Merkle tree hash over entry hashes (`blake3(0x00 || leaf)`, `blake3(0x01 || left || right)`).
- Nodes exchange `{from_seq, limit}` requests to catch up and compare roots; an entry that does not chain is refused.
- Facts: operator certificates, operator revocations, challenge failures, map version changes.
- Retention: forever (a few kilobytes per node per day; challenge failures may be aggregated per node and day at scale).

A root witnessed by many senior nodes cannot be rewritten without everyone noticing. The Console shows the latest
facts and the root.
